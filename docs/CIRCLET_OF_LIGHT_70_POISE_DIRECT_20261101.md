## New isolated Build equipment attribute preview
A separately gated preview recognizes **only** the double-read Circlet of Light Head item, when the opt-in source is loaded and the site-proven ID/name/slot/weight/Poise is uniquely selected. In Wretch all-10 with no other equipment, it presents the exact observed INT=11, FAI=11, ARC=11. Other base stats show the single observed bonus as an isolated arithmetic preview, **not** a certified full equipped Build result. At stat=99 (cap interaction unobserved), it withholds a final number. It does not propagate the bonus into AR, HP, damage, spell scaling or defense algorithms and it does not apply unproven item effects. Tests use the real Circlet data alongside invalid-name, missing-source, manual-provenance and stat-cap cases.
