Which roll tier applies?

Below 30% = Light; from 30% to below 70% = Medium; from 70% to below 100% = Heavy; 100% and above = Overloaded. Exactly on a threshold is the heavier category. Read the thresholds against your displayed in-game maximum, including active equipment-load modifiers.

Primary source: Bandai Namco: Advanced Stats.

Method

Equip-load thresholds

Light load is below 30%, medium is at least 30% but below 70%, heavy is at least 70% but below 100%, and overloaded is 100% or above.

Results are computed in your browser. See our calculation methods and verification status for data limitations.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Why should I enter the maximum load shown in-game?

Maximum load can change with attributes and effects. Using the current in-game limit avoids guessing missing item data.

Does the calculator know my armor inventory?

Not yet. This checker calculates load thresholds from the numbers you enter, without an unverified equipment database.
