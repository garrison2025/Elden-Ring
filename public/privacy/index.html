Privacy

These calculators are designed to run locally, without a sign-in or personal profile.

What this site processes

Inputs such as stats, rune budgets and equip load are processed in your own browser. The build share URL can encode your chosen class and eight attribute values; sharing that link shares those values with anyone who receives it.

Browser storage

The website may save calculator preferences in your browser. You can clear them using your browser's site-data controls. No game save files are requested or uploaded.

Third-party services

The static hosting provider necessarily receives standard connection metadata needed to deliver website files. No analytics, advertising networks, logins, payment services or third-party AI APIs are integrated in this version. This policy must be updated before introducing any of them.
