    Regular weapon upgrades

    +0 to +25

    For each grade from Smithing Stone [1] to [8], the three upgrade steps use 2, 4 and 6 stones. For +0 to +24 that's 12 stones per grade (96 total). The final +24 to +25 step uses one Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.

    Special weapon upgrades

    +0 to +10

    Somber weapons use one Somber Smithing Stone per level from [1] through [9], followed by one Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone for +10. Regular and somber upgrade tracks are not interchangeable.

    Source, scope and maintenance

    This schedule is based on published Smithing Stone and Somber Smithing Stone mechanics. See regular upgrade materials, somber upgrade materials and the upgrade-level reference.

    The tool does not calculate rune costs, where to obtain stones, game-specific drop rates, weapon stats, attack ratings or unavailable upgrades. Check official patches before relying on the schedule after major future game updates.
