Where do these numbers come from?

The factual fields are a compact derivative of the open-source Elden Ring API community dataset, source revision 1d2626c. Its API source declares MIT licensing, but this does not grant rights to unrelated game artwork, writing or proprietary assets. We exclude all source imagery, descriptions and lore text.

Three visibly inconsistent numerical entries were individually cross-checked with published weapon references. We also track 102 Shadow of the Erdtree weapons and shields across three disclosed data tiers: 18 with individually sourced +0 reference values, 52 with only known requirements, and 32 with names/categories only. Three weapons have community-published +0 to +10 base-attack tables. No reinforcement scaling coefficients, affinity profiles or total AR are certified. Other entries are not confirmed for the current patch. Source-code attribution and corrections are documented in the repository.

Why can't this calculate true AR yet?

Letter grades such as B or D cannot determine exact scaling coefficients, reinforcement tables or affinity changes. Many DLC records contain only names or requirements. None of these alone can determine a character's full attack rating or PvE damage.

For current official rules, see App 1.17.2 / Regulation 1.17.3. The separately maintained AR research workbench still requires authorized detailed parameters and independent in-game checks.
