Where do these numbers come from?

The factual fields are a compact derivative of the open-source Elden Ring API community dataset, source revision 1d2626c. Its API source declares MIT licensing, but this does not grant rights to unrelated game artwork, writing or proprietary assets. We exclude all source imagery, descriptions and lore text.

Three visibly inconsistent numerical entries were individually cross-checked with published weapon references. Other entries are not confirmed for the current patch. Source-code attribution and corrections are documented in the repository.

Why can't this calculate true AR yet?

Letter grades such as B or D cannot determine exact scaling coefficients, reinforcement tables or affinity changes. This reference only contains base (+0) details, not enough information to compute a character's full attack rating or compare PvE damage.

For current official rules, see App 1.17.2 / Regulation 1.17.3. The separately maintained AR research workbench still requires authorized detailed parameters and independent in-game checks.
