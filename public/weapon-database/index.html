Where do these numbers come from?

The factual fields in the original 305 unique base-game numeric rows (one identical duplicate Erdtree Seal source record was removed) are a compact derivative of the open-source Elden Ring API community dataset, source revision 1d2626c. Its API source declares MIT licensing, but this does not grant rights to unrelated game artwork, writing or proprietary assets. We exclude all source imagery, descriptions and lore text.

Three visibly inconsistent numerical entries were individually cross-checked with published weapon references. We track 102 Shadow of the Erdtree weapons and shields: 102 with +0 source-reported base-attack arrays (69 individual weapon pages, 33 wiki class comparison tables), including Stone-Sheathed Sword's disputed 89 +0 candidate versus 119 headline and conflicting 3.5 versus 5 weight; it remains excluded from trusted damage and load calculations. Three of the 101 records contain an explicitly unknown (?) scaling letter rather than a guessed grade. Six weapons have community-published +0 to +10 base-attack tables. No reinforcement scaling coefficients, affinity profiles or total AR are certified. Eight extra Tarnished Pack entries have named public roster corroboration and source-reported requirements and weights in a Regulation 1.17.3-labelled equipment reference; all eight have sourced community +0 attack, critical and letter grades (three individual wiki pages, four named wiki class table rows, and Ritual Thrusting Shield's separately corroborated 135 physical, critical 100, D/D scaling). Ritual's reported weight/STR requirements remain disputed among secondary sources, so load/wield checks remain blocked. The patch-labelled secondary site lists *max-upgrade* attack and is never used as +0. No game-client screenshot was reviewed. Other entries are not confirmed for the current patch. Source-code attribution and corrections are documented in the repository.

Why can't this calculate true AR yet?

Letter grades such as B or D cannot determine exact scaling coefficients, reinforcement tables or affinity changes. Many DLC records contain only names or requirements. None of these alone can determine a character's full attack rating or PvE damage.

For current official rules, see App 1.17.2 / Regulation 1.17.3. The separately maintained v1.17 reference AR workbench already offers an opt-in free public parameter source and local calculations. We now use Tarnished.dev v1.17 as a same-input numerical benchmark instead of demanding that visitors install the game. Before describing results as Tarnished.dev-benchmarked, we must actually record passing comparison cases. Current Regulation 1.17.3 game-client accuracy remains a separate, stronger claim.
