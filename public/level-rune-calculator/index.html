Method and limitations

The cost from level L to L+1 is based on the documented rune-cost expression, with integer rounding. Runes for a level interval are the sum of its individual steps. It does not include rune loss from death or temporary rune-gain buffs. If you enter runes per farming run, the run estimate uses the shortfall after your current budget and rounds up to a whole run. It does not predict actual farming speed.

Reference check: Almanauts rune data. Game version is the reference scope, not evidence of hands-on game-client verification.

Method

Rune budgeting

The tool applies a level cost function for each level-up and sums each step. Costs for buying items, upgrades and dropped rune recovery are not included.

Results are computed in your browser. See our calculation methods and verification status for data limitations.

Common questions

Frequently asked

What is the maximum level?

Level 713 is the maximum character level when all eight attributes are 99. You cannot advance to 714.

Are the results tied to the selected class?

Rune cost is based on current level, rather than starting class. Class choice affects your starting level and attribute minimums.
