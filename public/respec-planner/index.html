How the budget works

Your character's current level is its starting-class level plus all attribute points invested above the initial class minimums. A complete respec must redistribute those points without changing the level or violating the initial class minimums or the 99-point attribute cap.

Unspent points indicate an incomplete respec. A negative balance indicates the proposed stats require additional levels.

Limitations and privacy

This tool does not verify the availability of a respec item, access to the rebirth mechanic, temporary buffs, talismans, gear effects, weapon requirements or actual attack ratings. Numbers are calculated entirely within your browser.

The optional share URL encodes only your class and stats. See the accuracy record and privacy policy.
