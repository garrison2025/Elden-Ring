Weapon AR evidence comparator

Compare candidate parameter profiles against separately recorded weapon AR readings. Test exact values, reinforcement levels, one-hand/two-hand and damage components without using an API.

Not in-game verified. The initial example is completely fictional. Entered observations may be wrong; matching them is not proof of current regulation accuracy, licensing, or safe publication.

Uses the research engine's existing v1 schema. Missing reinforcement rows or correction curves are refused.

Release rules

Reports now include both rounding conventions for split-damage AR. If the alternate convention matches an observation, this identifies a candidate formula issue—not independently verified game accuracy.

Every case remains unreviewed until evidence and its game version are independently checked. Real weapon AR publication still requires documented data rights, complete affinity/upgrade information, current regulation support, in-game golden results and mobile usability QA.

This tool is never a substitute for client screenshots or rights review. Reports export directly to your computer; no server receives any file or value.
