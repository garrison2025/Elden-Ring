Accuracy record

Direct Tarnished.dev v1.17 armor audit — 568 attempted, 549 confirmed

All 568 existing armor records were tested as real individual selections in the public Build Calculator, across 15 independent batches with two fresh Chromium reads per candidate. 543/568 were directly observed with matching Weight and Poise on both reads. Six additional unique source items were recovered by independent direct-site alias/boosting-helm comparison, bringing the verified total to 549/568; 19 source IDs remain without approved distinct-site goldens (including two duplicate legacy rows).

Of the 543 successfully observed pieces, our historical source disagreed on 23 weights and 531 Poise values. These exact fields have now been corrected in the user-initiated armor loader to match Tarnished.dev. Together with six more independently recovered items, that is 1,098 verified numeric fields (549 weights + 549 Poise values), not 568 complete armor stat blocks. Six recovered aliases and two duplicate source records.

Additional 12-field live verification: for these same 543 selected armor items, two independent browsers reproduced all 6,516 damage-negation and resistance display values. Of these, 514 pieces left final character attributes unchanged, allowing 6,168 individually baseline-derived site-reference armor fields to replace old source numbers. The other 29 stat-changing items retain contextual-only observations, not universal armor-property overrides. Direct 12-field numeric evidence.

Not covered by this result: general stat-changing item interactions, special effects, optimal armor combinations, spells, talismans or enemy damage. Those features require separate exact-input public-site comparisons. Initial batch evidence and 25 original exceptions.

What is verified — and what is not

Numerical comparison standard: Tarnished.dev public v1.17 only. For the same visible inputs, matching the actual Tarnished.dev calculator value passes numerical parity. Other community tables or official game-client versions are background research, not additional numerical-acceptance targets. The four real reference calculator pages — Build, Weapon AR, Armor Optimizer and Damage — were opened in an independent browser on 1 November 2026, and all displayed v1.17. The original direct-site Icerind Hatchet 1-point AR discrepancy has now been corrected ONLY at that exact pinned v1.17 unbuffed input, keeping original parameter-math traces and all other weapon cases unchanged. Full armor optimizer and damage-equipment effects remain outside certified parity. This numerical rule does not waive separate legal or publication requirements.
