How this preview works

For each damage type: Attack Rating × selected motion value × user-entered multiplier → defense-ratio attenuation → enemy percentage negation. The five remaining amounts are added for damage per hit; estimated DPS multiplies by the user's attacks-per-second value.

Defense-ratio breakpoints are ⅛, 1, 2.5 and 8. Even at zero defense, the documented ratio model caps this stage at 90% rather than 100%. Split elemental AR is attenuated separately for each corresponding defense. This preview is grounded in Eldenpedia's damage explanation and the documented community ratio equations.

Not yet equivalent to Tarnished.dev v1.17: its Damage Calculator itself is marked a work in progress. This implementation does not yet choose actual moveset motion values, game enemy presets/NG scaling, critical or counter-hit multipliers, PvP, conditional buffs, status or damage-rounding steps. Do not treat the community model as independently checked same-input damage from Tarnished.dev.
