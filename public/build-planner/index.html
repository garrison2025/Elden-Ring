    How levels are calculated

    Once you set your attributes, use Find best starting class to compare origins for this exact build, or Plan rune costs to open the level calculator with your starting and target levels already filled in.

    Your build starts at the selected origin’s minimum stats. Its current level is the origin’s starting level plus the number of increased attribute points. The tool rejects stats below that starting class minimum and values above 99.

    Idus Knight and Heavy Knight are extra-content origins and remain hidden unless you explicitly enable Tarnished Pack. Shadow of the Erdtree is a separate expansion: its weapons have their own availability switch. The optional weapon section shows cited reference facts and requirement feasibility only, not simulated attack or complete equipped load.

    How to plan an Elden Ring character without wasting levels

    1. Start with your actual origin. Choose one of the ten original starting classes or explicitly enable the two Tarnished Pack origins. Minimum base attributes are locked: switching origins resets the local draft to the new class minimums.
    2. Allocate all eight attributes. Each point above that origin's base values adds one character level; the resulting level and rune cost are calculated locally. Character attributes cannot exceed 99, and level cannot exceed 713.
    3. Check your weapon constraints separately. Search an optional community-referenced weapon, inspect its five minimum attributes, and compare one-hand versus eligible two-hand Strength requirements. Unknown and disputed source fields stay labelled and never become exact combat claims.
    4. Compare before committing. Pin Build A, edit Build B, then review the attribute delta, level delta and rune difference. If you want a real equipped-weight tier, open Equip Load and enter your actual in-game maximum and equipped weights instead of assuming all shortlisted weapons are equipped.
    5. Share or save intentionally. Share links encode build choices in the URL; optional local saving stays in your browser and has an explicit delete control. Neither operation sends your save game or third-party regulation data to our servers.

    Reference scope: Tarnished.dev Build Calculator (public v1.17 reference) is the comparison baseline for calculation workflows, not a guarantee that every third-party weapon entry or effect is independently game-verified. Read source and accuracy boundaries.

    Common questions

    Frequently asked

    Why is a build level higher than expected?

    A class begins with its own level and minimum eight attributes. Values below its starting minimum are not valid; adding ten attribute points raises the resulting level by ten.

    Can I share my build?

    Yes. Copy the share URL containing the starting class and eight stat values. It contains no personal account information.
