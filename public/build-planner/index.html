How levels are calculated

Once you set your attributes, use Find best starting class to compare origins for this exact build, or Plan rune costs to open the level calculator with your starting and target levels already filled in.

Your build starts at the selected origin’s minimum stats. Its current level is the origin’s starting level plus the number of increased attribute points. The tool rejects stats below that starting class minimum and values above 99.

Idus Knight and Heavy Knight are extra-content origins and remain hidden unless you explicitly enable Tarnished Pack. Shadow of the Erdtree is a separate expansion: its weapons have their own availability switch. The optional weapon section shows cited reference facts and requirement feasibility only, not simulated attack or complete equipped load.

Method

Build Stats

A build planner helps you estimate level cost before reallocating stats. It does not currently calculate equipped weapons, damage, spell scaling or buffs.

Results are computed in your browser. See our calculation methods and verification status for data limitations.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Why is a build level higher than expected?

A class begins with its own level and minimum eight attributes. Values below its starting minimum are not valid; adding ten attribute points raises the resulting level by ten.

Can I share my build?

Yes. Copy the share URL containing the starting class and eight stat values. It contains no personal account information.
