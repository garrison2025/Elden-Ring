What does Attack Rating mean?

For pinned v1.17 parameter data, nominal AR is the integer floor of the sum of unrounded physical and elemental attack components. Consequently, the displayed total may be 1 higher than the sum of individually floored component cells. This rule matches 50/50 twice-observed Tarnished.dev v1.17 website cases. AR adds the weapon's physical and elemental attack components before a target's defense and negation. A higher combined AR does not always mean higher damage per hit, especially for split damage types. This calculator does not simulate motion values, buffs, status buildup, weapon skills, spell scaling, or enemy defenses.

Version distinction: these calculations use opt-in v1.17 community parameters. The 50 independently repeated Tarnished.dev v1.17 examples validate representative input/output behavior; they do not certify all weapons or the newer Regulation 1.17.3. For research exports and raw-math audits, use the advanced local weapon data workbench.
