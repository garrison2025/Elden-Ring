What does Attack Rating mean?

AR adds the weapon's physical and elemental attack components before a target's defense and negation. A higher combined AR does not always mean higher damage per hit, especially for split damage types. This calculator does not simulate motion values, buffs, status buildup, weapon skills, spell scaling, or enemy defenses.

Version distinction: these calculations use opt-in v1.17 community parameters. The 15 matching Tarnished.dev examples validate representative input/output behavior; they do not certify all weapons or the newer Regulation 1.17.3. For research exports and raw-math audits, use the advanced local weapon data workbench.
