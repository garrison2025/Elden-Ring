Rules

Why dual-grip Strength matters

Standard two-handing boosts effective STR by 50%, rounded down for this calculation. For instance, 16 STR meets a 24 STR weapon requirement. DEX, INT, FAI and ARC never gain the same boost.

The game publisher's Advanced Stats guide confirms the standard 1.5× Strength rule and its 24/16 example. Certain paired and special weapon behaviors are exceptions; disable the boost where appropriate.

Limitations

No attack rating claims

Meeting stat requirements does not calculate actual weapon damage, attack rating, skill scaling, buffs, stat-boosting equipment, affinities or upgrading costs. This is a minimum stat planner, not a substitute for verified weapon data.

All inputs are processed on your device. The optional URL contains only numerical attributes. See our accuracy policy and privacy policy.
